I made a soldering iron fume extractor to help protect me from harmful gases that are released during the soldering process through flux being melted. I took an old Foxconn DC brushless fan from an old computer that takes in 12 V, 0.24 A, and got it a wall adapter that outputs 12 V, 1 A. With simple wiring, I connected the fan's positive and negative wires to a DC female plug that connects to the wall adapter. Then I  soldered everything together and put heat shrinks on. It sucks air away from my face, not eliminating all danger but at least some, and it can be upgraded with a charcoal-enabled filter and a 3D-printed stand.
